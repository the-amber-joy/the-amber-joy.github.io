    Blogtober - Day 1

    How long can I keep this up?

    Thursday, Oct 1, 2026 Updated Oct 3, 2026
    #blogtober2026 #botober2026

    Blogtober

    This morning someone at work mentioned Blogtober, which is just a challenge to post something every day of the month. This is just an old-fashioned Web 1.0 personal diary-type blog, no real specific topic or “lifestyle” (ugh), just me chattering about stuff. The typical blogger today has articles they can pre-write and prepare in advance to post daily, but that’s not me. Still, I’ll give it a shot. Let’s see how many days in a row I can go before I forget to post something!

    Today is just a brain dump. Here we go…

    Foggy Sunset

    We walk Penny around the path at the park across the street every evening. It’s exactly 1 mile if we loop the park once, and the puppy loves it (we do too!). It’s always very pretty down there, but last night there was an especially cool fog during sunset. T tried to capture it but photos really don’t do justice for how otherworldly it looked! Very spooky.

    Workiversary

    Last Friday marked 3 years for me at my current company. T and I were both working from home that day, and I didn’t think anything of it when he ran a quick errand around lunchtime. When he got back, I was focused on my computer and didn’t see him come in, but I heard him say “Happy Workiversary!” so I replied, “Yep that’s today!” withut turning around. He said, “I’ll just put this over here,” and that’s when I finally looked to see what he was doing.

    He had picked up a bouquet of fall flowers, trimmed them, put them in a vase, and set them on the coffee table in the office! Work anniversaries aren’t much of a big deal for us, especially since I’ve spent the last several years bouncing around beween different startups, but he remembered I had mentioned it in passing a few days earlier, and decided to do this nice little surprise for me. So sweet!

    They’re still looking pretty good several days later!

    Botober

    Finally, it’s Botober again. Janelle Shane has a wonderful blog called AI Weirdness which I’ve loved for years, and every year since 2019 she’s had one of her models produce a list of art prompts for the month of October, in the vein of Inktober and other October art challenges. This year she had a tiny model generate art prompts based on a list of 42,000 registered racehorse names from the Jockey Club. The results are charmingly hilarious and frequently nonsensical.

    I think blogging daily is enough of a challenge, but trying to draw or paint something each day might be fun, too! I’m not committing to it yet, but with prompts like “Lady Attackpot” and “Speed Puddin” it seems like it’d be awfully fun.

    Updated Oct 3 to add the pic I drew for Oct 1

    I ususally like to quickly doodle my October drawings in under 3 minutes and just see what happens when I go fast and don’t question much of what I’m doing. It has a cake!
