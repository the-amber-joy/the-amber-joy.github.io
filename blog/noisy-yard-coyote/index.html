    Another coyote in the yard

    So noisy

    Tuesday, Sep 29, 2026
    #wildlife

    I was having trouble falling asleep last night (again)… (ok, always). After an hour or so of tossing and turning, I had just about drifted off, when I heard through the open window a squeaky-sounding howl, followed by some yappy barks. It startled me, and I was so sleepy and confused, I thought it was the neighbor’s beagle at first! Then I realized it didn’t sound anything like that dog, but more like a coyote. We’ve had them around here before - just last winter, I saw one trotting across the cul-de-sac late at night.

    T was in the basement working, and didn’t hear anything. Penny was sleeping in her kennel at the foot of the bed, and didn’t make a peep - I wonder if she even heard it. I’m glad she didn’t get excited though. If she did, she’d probably think it was time to wake up, which means she gets to snuggle in the Big Bed, and frankly I just can’t handle that. For a 25-pound dog, she is a surprisingly aggressive kicker!
