    Cozy Day Off

    I may be slacking on this blog but I'm still having a nice October

    Thursday, Oct 15, 2026

    Where have I been?

    Apparently I fell off of both #blogtober and #botober pretty quickly, but that’s OK. I realized I don’t have any reason to blog every day or even draw every day, because I’m still happy just doing my own thing, what I like, when I like.

    How are the kids?

    The older kid is still off at college of course, and it sounds like he’s doing well, having fun, and visiting my mom’s house regularly since she just lives across the river. The younger kid is at Dads’ for the long weekend (kids in MN get a couple extra days off in October so their teachers can attend the MEA Conference). He’s been collecting supplies to make a new Halloween costume, and is hoping to work on that a bit over the weekend, so I’m eager to see how much progress is made!

    We made a leaf pile for the puppy

    Younger kid wanted to rake some leaves into a pile last weekend, just to play with before we mowed & mulched them into the lawn. So we raked up a nice little pile, and then threw dog toys in it for Penny to find. It was super cute and we all had a lot of fun!

    What am I up to for the long weekend?

    I had Monday off work for Indigenous Peoples Day, and I took Thursday and Friday off so I could enjoy a long weekend for myself just like the kids.

    Maximum Cozy, movies, and tea

    It’s been gray and rainy, which is perfect for curling up by the fire in my softest sweatpants with all the blankets. I love that I can hear the rain on the roof in my den. T is here too, he took today and tomorrow off like I did, but he has to do a bunch of personal paperwork that he couldn’t get done during the work week, so he doesn’t get to revel in relaxation like I am today!

    We did watch Coyote vs Acme last night, which was really charming and fun. I have avoided as many spoilers and info as possible, so I only knew the basic gist of it, but I never expected it to be as philosophical and tear-jerking as it was. Oh yeah, I absolutely cried, and by the end it was impossible to imagine Sisyphus as anything but happy. And the boulder is probably happy, too.

    So today, while T is doing his paperwork, I’ve been curled up by the fire with a cup of tea (it’s a perfect day for Lavender Earl Grey with a few drops of vanilla, and milk & sugar to make a London Fog). I did a little knitting too, and baked a big pan of brownies with some pecans I found at the back of the freezer.

    I painted my nails, too

    I actually did this a couple days ago, but I like how minimalist these orange tips look!

    I might wipe them off and do something else tonight. T just got back from a grocery run, and surprised me with some Peanut Butter Porter, so I might make a little charcuterie tray, have a beer, and give myself a manicure by the fireplace.
