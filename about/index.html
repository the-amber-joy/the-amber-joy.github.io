    About Me

    One of the first things we like to ask about people is what they do for a living. I don't feel like I'm defined as a person by what I do for money. My paycheck is just a way to fund the life I enjoy during the rest of my hours away from work. That being said, I work as a software engineer, and I also enjoy coding for fun in my free time, among other things. This isn't a tech blog, but tech may certainly come up once in a while!

    I'm a mom to a couple teenagers, one in middle school and the other in college. I won't post pictures of them or use their names without their permission.

    I have a lovely fella in my life, Terrence. He's a kind, steadfast and loving companion, and a wonderful step-dad to my kids. We dated for a while in college, but some complications happened (he got deployed to war, I got married to someone else, yadda yadda yadda)... and this led to us being separated. Several years later, I was divorced, he was single, and we cautiously reconnected. We've been back together for over 10 years now and I couldn't ask for a better partner in life.

    We have a puppy, Penny, a scrappy little terrier mix. As of this writing (September, 2026), she is 19 months old. I think we'll consider her a puppy until she's at least 3 years old. Maybe forever.
